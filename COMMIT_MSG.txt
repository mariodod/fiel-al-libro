Art polish: the Venture's two faces, the dummy, and the straw man

Rebuilt the Venture's two forms with better proportions, materials, and
detail, all in the game's gouache-on-plaster palette:

- windmill_reality.tscn: The mill tower now has stone course bands (dark
  and light ochre rings around the cylinder) to suggest masonry, and the
  outer sail strips use a torn canvas material. The low doorway and
  lintel are kept as the weak point.
- windmill_book.tscn: The giant is taller and gaunter, with better
  proportions (thighs, shins, feet, hips, belt, chest). Its arms end in
  tattered sail-hands with torn canvas strips, and its head is the
  millstone with a drilled hole.
- training_dummy.gd: Added a stuffed-straw body cylinder with a waist
  tie, darker arm-end block, and weathered wood material.
- straw_man.gd: Added a straw hat (brim + crown) on the head, darker
  arm-end blocks, and a slightly taller posture.
