# Chapter 4 — The Encinados

> Region II · Sierra Morena · Chapter 4 of 13
> Venture: The Encinados

## Summary

Bandits in the dry mountains. The Encina(dos) are mounted highwaymen who read as Book-knights or Reality-bandits depending on truth. First mounted combat encounter. Introduces mounts as a mechanic (Tier 2 — nice, not essential).

## Location

**Region II: Sierra Morena** — dry mountain, bandits, caves.

## Venture: The Encinados

Mounted combat. The bandits ride; the player can fight on foot or acquire a mount (Rocinante for Quixote, Dapple for Sancho — Tier 2, optional).

## Characters

- **Quixote**: lance, charge attack on mount
- **Sancho**: flail, dismount attack

## Colophon

The author reflects on how bandits become knights in the telling, and knights become bandits in the retelling.
