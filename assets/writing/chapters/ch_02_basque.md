# Chapter 2 — The Basque

> Region I · El Camino de la Mancha · Chapter 2 of 13
> Venture: El Escudero del Cuartel (tutorial Venture)

## Summary

The player's first real combat encounter. A Basque and his three lackeys at a roadside inn — a clash of perspectives. Quixote reads it as a knightly challenge; Sancho reads it as a drunken brawl. Teaches parry, lock-on, and stamina management.

## Location

**Region I: El Camino de la Mancha** — roadside inn, the same Posada de Villanueva or a roadside stop.

## Venture: El Escudero del Cuartel

A tutorial-level boss fight. Four enemies (one Basque, three lackeys). The player can fight them all or use the truth toggle to make some disappear (in Book truth they are knights; in Reality truth they are peasants).

## Characters

- **Quixote**: cannot parry, lance, full damage
- **Sancho**: can parry, sack + flail, reduced damage

## Colophon

The author comments on how the same scene reads as chivalry or as brawl, depending on who is telling it.
