# Chapter 11 — Knight of the Mirrors

> Region V · El Castillo del Duque · Chapter 11 of 13
> Venture: Knight of the Mirrors

## Summary

Sansón Carrasco, who beat Quixote "by the book." A late-game mirror boss that mechanically forces the two-truths read. The knight fights with book-knowledge; the player must use truth toggling to find the opening.

## Location

**Region V: El Castillo del Duque** — false chivalry, silk, mirrors, theatre. The hall of mirrors.

## Venture: Knight of the Mirrors

A mirror-match boss. The knight copies the player's moves. The player must toggle truth to create a difference — in Book truth the knight has one pattern; in Reality truth another. The opening appears only when the two truths disagree.

## Characters

- **Quixote**: fights the mirror-knight
- **Sancho**: supports, can parry the mirror's attacks

## Colophon

The author writes about fighting oneself — and how every mirror shows a different truth.
