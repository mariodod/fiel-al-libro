# Chapter 3 — The Canting Sea

> Region I · El Camino de la Mancha · Chapter 3 of 13
> Venture: Mar de los Cantabros (weather boss)

## Summary

The sea as a raging monster; the ship as a galleon. A "boss" that is weather — wind, waves, rain. The player must navigate the storm while the world flips between Book (a living sea-monster) and Reality (bad weather). Introduces environmental hazards and non-humanoid Ventures.

## Location

**Region I: El Camino de la Mancha** — dry riverbed that reads as sea in Book truth.

## Venture: Mar de los Cantabros

**Book form — The Sea Monster:** the dry riverbed is an ocean; waves crash; the galleon is a real ship. The player fights the sea itself.

**Reality form — Bad Weather:** wind pushes the player, rain reduces visibility, the riverbed is just dry earth. The fight is about positioning and endurance.

## Characters

- **Quixote**: sees the sea monster, full damage
- **Sancho**: sees the weather, reduced damage, can parry

## Colophon

The author writes about how imagination turns a dry riverbed into an ocean — and how the ocean was never there at all.
