# Chapter 6 — The Bridge

> Region II · Sierra Morena · Chapter 6 of 13
> Venture: El Puente de los Mundos (psychological-horror level)

## Summary

Quixote on a narrow plank, imagining whole worlds across it. A psychological-horror level: a bridge over a chasm that reads as solid stone in Book truth and as rotting wood in Reality truth. The player must cross while the world flips under their feet.

## Location

**Region II: Sierra Morena** — a high mountain pass, a narrow bridge over a ravine.

## Venture: El Puente de los Mundos

**Book form:** solid stone bridge, wide, safe. The chasm below is mist.
**Reality form:** rotting wooden planks, narrow, dangerous. The chasm below is real and deep.

The player crosses while toggling truth. If the truth flips while on the wrong form, the planks give way.

## Characters

- **Quixote**: trusts the bridge in Book truth
- **Sancho**: distrusts the bridge in Reality truth

## Colophon

The author writes about bridges between worlds — and how some bridges only exist because someone believed in them.
