# Chapter 12 — The Magician

> Region V · El Castillo del Duque · Chapter 12 of 13
> Venture: The Magician

## Summary

A court magician who manipulates the truth layer directly. The Venture can flip truth without the player's input, creating sections where the player must fight in a truth they did not choose. Introduces involuntary truth flips as a combat mechanic.

## Location

**Region V: El Castillo del Duque** — the magician's tower, full of illusions.

## Venture: The Magician

The magician can toggle truth at will. The player must adapt mid-fight: a parry window that exists in Book truth may not exist in Reality truth. The magician's health is low but he is hard to pin down.

## Characters

- **Quixote**: vulnerable to involuntary truth flips
- **Sancho**: Desvelar can strip the magician's illusions

## Colophon

The author writes about how a magician's trick is just a truth that hasn't been examined closely enough.
