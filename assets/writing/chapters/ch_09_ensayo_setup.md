# Chapter 9 — The Ensayo (Setup)

> Region IV · Los Riscos de Medinaceli · Chapter 9 of 13
> No Venture · No combat · Ensayo Phase 1

## Summary

The first of two Ensayo chapters (Region IV). The Curious Impertinent (I.50). The player does not read about this — the player stages it. A test with three sliders and one question. Each choice is legal; the game does not judge the setup — it judges the outcome.

## Location

**Region IV: Los Riscos de Medinaceli** — sheer cliffs, a "Gothic" church, fog. The Ensayo takes place in the church.

## Phase 1 — The Setup

**THE TEST:**
> A knight must prove that he can be trusted with a secret.
> He does not know that another knight has been sent to take it from him.

| Slider | Options |
|--------|---------|
| Who plays the tested? | Quixote | Sancho |
| Who plays the examiner? | Quixote | Sancho |
| What is the secret? | the wife's name | the treasure | the truth |
| Who is told the truth? | the tested | the examiner | neither |

Each choice constructs the conditions under which events will happen. The game records the setup in the Manifiesto.

## Characters

- **The Tested**: the one being tested
- **The Examiner**: the one running the test
- **AI motive**: the non-player character acts on motive (curiosity, jealousy, love, need to be important, need to be right, fear of being thought a fool)

## Colophon

The author writes: "This is the story I wish I had written" — and then lets the player write it instead.
