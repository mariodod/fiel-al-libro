# Chapter 5 — Montesinos

> Region II · Sierra Morena · Chapter 5 of 13
> Venture: La Cueva de los Sueños (non-combat surreal Venture)

## Summary

The Cave of Montesinos — a dream cave; a promise, a knight, a girl waiting for a "false" hero. Non-combat, surreal. The player enters a cave that reads differently in each truth: Book truth shows a magical adventure; Reality truth shows a damp cave with bats. Introduces non-combat Ventures and the idea that not everything is a fight.

## Location

**Region II: Sierra Morena** — the Cave of Montesinos, deep in the mountains.

## Venture: La Cueva de los Sueños

No combat. The player explores, reads murals, meets dream-figures. The cave's geometry shifts between truths. A puzzle Venture.

## Characters

- **Quixote**: believes the dream is real, sees the knight and the girl
- **Sancho**: sees bats and damp stone, knows it's a cave

## Colophon

The author writes about dreams and how they feel more real than waking life — until you wake up.
