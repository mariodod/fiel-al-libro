# Chapter 7 — Dulcinea

> Region III · El Toboso & Montes de Toledo · Chapter 7 of 13
> Venture: La Encañada (break the illusion)

## Summary

A peasant girl sold as an enchantress; the "verisimilitude" of the illusion. Quixote sees Dulcinea, a noble lady; Sancho sees Aldonza, a farm girl. The player must break the illusion and see what's underneath — or choose not to.

## Location

**Region III: El Toboso & Montes de Toledo** — slow river country, the "enchanted" village of Toboso.

## Venture: La Encañada

**Book form — Dulcinea:** a noble lady, radiant, graceful. Speaking to her advances Quixote's quest.
**Reality form — Aldonza:** a farm girl, plain, confused. She does not know who Quixote is.

The player chooses which truth to accept. Breaking the illusion costs Humanity if done cruelly.

## Characters

- **Quixote**: sees Dulcinea, cannot parry, full damage
- **Sancho**: sees Aldonza, can parry, reduced damage
- **Aldonza/Dulcinea**: NPC, the same person read two ways

## Colophon

The author writes about how a name can make a princess of a peasant girl — and how the name is all that remains when the illusion breaks.
