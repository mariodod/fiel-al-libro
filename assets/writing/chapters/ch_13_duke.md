# Chapter 13 — The Duke

> Region V · El Castillo del Duque · Chapter 13 of 13
> Venture: The Duke

## Summary

The final chapter. The Duke — false chivalry, silk, theatre — is the culmination of everything the player has learned. The final Venture. After this, the ending is determined by tracked variables: BooksCollected, QuixoteLocura, SanchoLocura, Humanity, Verdicts, TheSecondBook.

## Location

**Region V: El Castillo del Duque** — the castle, the throne room, the final stage.

## Venture: The Duke

A multi-phase boss fight. The Duke uses every mechanic the player has seen: truth flips, Desvelar, parry, lock-on, stamina. The fight tests everything.

## Endings (GDD.md §12.2)

Three main endings determined by tracked variables:

1. **La Locura Compartida** — both characters high Locura. "Happy" ending: the world is the book, both are knights. Also the "worse" ending.
2. **El Mundo Real** — both cured, the book shelved. The saddest ending.
3. **El Manuscrito** — hidden ending: the player is the reader, the game is the book, the playthrough is the second book. Unlocked by collecting the Codex and reaching a specific page.

## Colophon

The final colophon: the author closes the book. In Book Truth, the adventure continues. In Reality Truth, it ends here.
