# Chapter 1 — The Windmills

> Region I · El Camino de la Mancha · Chapter 1 of 13
> Venture: Culebras de los Molinos (the windmill boss)

## Summary

The player's first encounter with a Venture. Quixote sees giants where Sancho sees windmills. The truth toggle (T) flips the world between Book (giants) and Reality (mills). Sancho's Desvelar (X) can strip the Book skin from the mill, revealing its Reality form mid-fight.

## Location

**Region I: El Camino de la Mancha** — open wheat plains, a few windmills, the first inn (Posada de Villanueva).

## Posada

**Posada de Villanueva** — the starting hub. Checkpoint, shelf, innkeeper, musician. The player meets Quixote and Sancho here before setting out.

## Venture: Culebras de los Molinos

**Book form — The Giant:** walks, reaches further than the player's roll, fast and dangerous. Half the windmill's health. Short, intense fight.

**Reality form — The Mill:** does not walk. Sails turn. Ground within four metres of the tower is not yours (rotating gear-and-stone hazard). Topples when least expected, long recovery. Fight of position: come close, hit, be somewhere else.

**Truth resolution:** killing in Book truth → TRUTH verdict → grants Manifiesto fragment `bk.ch01.molinos.01`. Killing in Reality truth → FORCED verdict → no fragment, different colophon.

## Anamorphoses (optional)

None mandatory. One optional anamorphosis: from a specific vantage point on the road, a rock reads as a book page (the "page in the rock").

## Characters

- **Quixote** (player, default): cannot parry, full damage, lance
- **Sancho** (companion AI): can parry, reduced damage, sack + flail
- **Innkeeper** (NPC at Posada): gives the windmill rumor, saves the game

## Colophon

After the chapter, a page of the 1605 text: the author's voice. In Book Truth, the colophon agrees that the giants were real. In Reality Truth, it corrects: they were windmills all along. Occasionally wrong on purpose — a clue that reality has shifted.

## Truth Layer Implications

- First time the player experiences the world changing under them
- Teaches that T (Quixote) and X (Sancho) are different levers belonging to different characters
- The Venture's form depends on whose truth is active when the fight starts
